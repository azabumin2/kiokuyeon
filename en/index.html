From consultation to marriage

You take each step together with our staff.

  1. 1

    Personal consultation

    Tell us about the life you hope for, your views on marriage and any worries.

  2. 2

    Document check & registration

    We check the required documents, such as identity and single status, before you register.

  3. 3

    Introductions

    Our staff introduce partners based on values and plans for the future, not only on conditions.

  4. 4

    First meeting

    When you both would like to meet, we arrange an online or in-person meeting.

  5. 5

    Dating support

    Whenever language or culture gets confusing, just ask us.

  6. 6

    Preparing to marry

    We guide you through preparation and, where needed, refer you to specialists for procedures.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Korean or Japanese?

No. While dating you can also ask us about meaning and how to express your feelings. Our staff’s languages and hours will be posted on this site once confirmed.

Who runs this service?

미래결혼정보 서울, a registered international marriage agency in Jongno-gu, Seoul. Registration and insurance details are on the Operator page.

What is checked before registration?

We check the required documents, such as identity documents and documents showing that you are single. We will tell you which ones during your consultation.

Will other members see my profile?

No. Only you and our staff can see it. Even when we introduce someone, we do not share names or contact details.
