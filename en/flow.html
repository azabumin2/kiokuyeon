How It Works & Fees

From consultation to marriage

You take each step together with our staff.

  1. 1

    Personal consultation

    Tell us about the life you hope for, your views on marriage and any worries.

  2. 2

    Document check & registration

    We check the required documents, such as identity and single status, before you register.

  3. 3

    Introductions

    Our staff introduce partners based on values and plans for the future, not only on conditions.

  4. 4

    First meeting

    When you both would like to meet, we arrange an online or in-person meeting.

  5. 5

    Dating support

    Whenever language or culture gets confusing, just ask us.

  6. 6

    Preparing to marry

    We guide you through preparation and, where needed, refer you to specialists for procedures.
