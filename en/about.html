About Kiokuyeon

Memory and bond

What our name means to us.

“Gieok” (기억) means memory in Korean, and “yeon” (연) means a bond between people.
The day you first met, the words you had to say again and again, the small moments you laughed together. Memories like these slowly grow into a lasting bond. That is the idea behind our name.

Korea and Japan. Two people born in different places and raised in different languages get to know each other’s culture, meet, date, marry and build a life together. We are there from the very first step.

We never judge personality or views on marriage by nationality. We start by listening carefully to each person’s values, sincerity and the life they hope for.

What we care about

Seeing each person

Values, sincerity and plans for the future matter as much as nationality or conditions.

Conveying feelings

Beyond translating words, we help the feelings behind them come across.

Honest and clear

We publish our registration and insurance details and are open about what we can and cannot do.
