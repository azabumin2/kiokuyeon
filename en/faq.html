FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Korean or Japanese?

No. While dating you can also ask us about meaning and how to express your feelings. Our staff’s languages and hours will be posted on this site once confirmed.

Who runs this service?

미래결혼정보 서울, a registered international marriage agency in Jongno-gu, Seoul. Registration and insurance details are on the Operator page.

What is checked before registration?

We check the required documents, such as identity documents and documents showing that you are single. We will tell you which ones during your consultation.

Will other members see my profile?

No. Only you and our staff can see it. Even when we introduce someone, we do not share names or contact details.

How much does it cost?

Our service is completely free of charge for women members. For men members, fees are explained during the personal consultation.

Can my family join the consultation?

Yes. We welcome questions from family members too. Please mention it in your request.

Do you help with marriage procedures?

We guide you through preparation and, where needed, refer you to specialists. We do not act on your behalf for legal procedures or guarantee their outcome.

What should I send in the consultation form?

Just a name we can call you, your country or region, age group, preferred contact method and details, and your question. Please do not send ID, income or health information through the form.
